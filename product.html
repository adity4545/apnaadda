The Marlowe is our answer to the throwaway winter coat. Cut from a 640gsm pure Italian wool melton, it holds its shape through the wettest, greyest months and only softens with wear. The line is relaxed but deliberate — clean shoulders, a gentle drape and a length that sits just below the knee.

Made to last

  • 100% pure Italian wool, 640gsm
  • Full cupro lining, breathable
  • Natural horn buttons
  • Deep welt hip pockets
  • Fully taped inner seams
  • Made in a family mill in Biella

Care

Dry clean only. Brush down after wear and store on a wide wooden hanger to keep the shoulders sharp. A steam refresh between cleans keeps the wool looking its best.

The Marlowe is designed for a relaxed layering fit. If you're between sizes or plan to wear it over chunky knits, we'd suggest sizing up. The model is 178cm and wears a size M.
